## Options considered

### Option A: Token Bases

Token based limit by model, setting the text lenght based on the model. 
Pros: It better reflects the cost/capacity of the LLM.
Cons: Limited by model, changing models might be more work and it is subject to providers changes. Tokens require tokenization or an estimate.

### Option B: Meeting averega words by time lenght

Maximum lenght based on average meeting transcription size and token based
Pros: Limit size is dynamic depending of time rather than model capabilities
Cons: Words do not accurately represent the cost of an LLM. It also depends on user input.

Example:
- 15-minute meeting: 1,750–2,250 words
- 30-minute meeting: 3,500–4,500 words
- 60-minute meeting: 7,000–9,000 words
- 90-minute meeting: 10,500–13,500 words

### Option C: Fixed value
Limit size is fixed regardless of model
Pros: It is simple.
Cons: Doesn't take in account tokens, meeting size, or anything.

## Decision

We chose option b because it allows us to offer a wide range of limits based on practical information, as well as not depending on the limitations of a specific model.

## Consequences

Define the size of meetings the system can handle, which influences UX, costs, and the future need for hierarchical chunking/summarization. Setting a very low limit may prevent some types of meetings, while setting a higher limit doesn't really have a negative impact other than the cost of the model if it were ever used.
