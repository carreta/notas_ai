# TD-017 — Add meeting time input for relative date interpretation

## Status

Proposed

## Context

The AI analysis may need to interpret relative dates and times mentioned during a meeting, such as "tomorrow", "next Monday", "in two weeks", or "last Friday". Without knowing when the meeting actually took place, the AI must infer the reference point for these relative expressions. This can lead to inaccurate absolute dates in the generated analysis, actions, deadlines, and follow-ups.

A dedicated `meeting time` input would provide the AI with an explicit temporal reference point, allowing relative dates to be resolved more reliably.

Affected areas:

- Meeting submission and validation.
- AI analysis contract and date interpretation.
- Action/deadline representation.
- Meeting creation UI/API.

## Constraints

- The meeting time must provide an unambiguous temporal reference for AI date interpretation.
- The value should be compatible with the application's timezone/date handling strategy.
- The field should not prevent meeting submission when the exact meeting time is unknown.
- Existing meeting data and workflows should remain compatible.
- AI analysis should explicitly account for the absence of a meeting time rather than silently assuming an arbitrary reference date.

## Options considered

### Option A

Add an optional `meeting time` input to the meeting submission.

**Pros:**

- Provides an explicit reference point for resolving relative dates.
- Improves accuracy of AI-generated action deadlines and dates.
- Does not block users who do not know the exact meeting time.
- Can be incorporated into the AI application contract.

---

**Cons:**

- Adds an additional input to the meeting creation flow.
- Requires validation, persistence, and timezone handling.
- AI behavior must account for cases where the field is omitted.

### Option B

Do not collect meeting time and let the AI infer the reference date from the available context.

**Pros:**

- Simpler user interface.
- No additional database field or validation logic.
- Less information required from the user.

---


**Cons:**

- Relative dates may be resolved incorrectly.
- AI may use the analysis/request timestamp instead of the actual meeting date.
- Results become less deterministic and harder to validate.
- Incorrect dates can lead to incorrect action deadlines and follow-ups.

## Decision

Add an **optional `meeting time` input*- to the meeting submission flow. When provided, the meeting time will be passed to the AI analysis as the authoritative temporal reference for interpreting relative dates.

When the field is not provided, the system must treat the reference time as unknown and the AI analysis must avoid presenting inferred absolute dates as certain. The absence of the meeting time should be represented explicitly in the AI application contract.

This approach provides improved date accuracy when information is available while preserving the ability to submit meetings where the exact time is unknown.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Relative dates can be converted into accurate absolute dates when the meeting time is available.
- AI-generated actions and deadlines become more deterministic.
- The AI contract has an explicit temporal reference.
- Users are not forced to provide a meeting time when it is unavailable.
- Date interpretation can be covered with deterministic test cases.

### Negative

- The meeting input and persistence model require an additional field.
- Validation and timezone handling become part of the implementation.
- UI/API consumers must support the new optional field.
- AI analysis needs separate behavior for meetings with and without a meeting time.

### Testing / operations / team workflow

Tests should cover:

- Meeting submission with a valid meeting time.
- Meeting submission without a meeting time.
- Invalid meeting-time formats.
- Relative dates resolved using a known meeting time.
- Relative dates when the meeting time is unavailable.
- Timezone-sensitive date interpretation.

## Compatibility / migration

Add an optional `meeting_time` field to the meetings persistence model and update the meeting submission contract accordingly. The field should be nullable to preserve compatibility with existing meetings and clients that do not provide a meeting time.

Existing records do not require backfilling unless a reliable meeting time can be determined from existing data. The AI application contract should represent the meeting time as optional and explicitly distinguish between a known and unknown temporal reference.
